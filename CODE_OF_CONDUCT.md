# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

cfetch welcomes people who want to improve the project in good faith. Be
precise, patient, and respectful. Critique ideas, implementations, and evidence;
do not attack people.

Expected conduct includes:

- giving technical feedback that is specific and actionable;
- acknowledging uncertainty and correcting mistakes openly;
- respecting privacy, attribution, licensing, and security boundaries; and
- helping keep discussions useful to contributors with different backgrounds.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, sexualized attention, doxxing,
deliberate intimidation, sustained disruption, spam, and knowingly false claims
about another participant are unacceptable.

## Scope

This policy applies in the repository, issue tracker, pull requests, project
discussions, release channels, and private communication undertaken on behalf of
the project.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns confidentially through the repository's
[private reporting form](https://github.com/corbet-labs/cfetch/security/advisories/new)
and prefix the title with `[conduct]`. Do not expose private evidence in a public
issue. If a report concerns the sole maintainer, use
[GitHub's abuse-reporting process](https://support.github.com/contact/report-abuse).

The maintainers may remove content, issue a private or public warning, restrict
participation temporarily, or ban a participant permanently. Decisions consider
severity, intent, repetition, remediation, and the safety of affected people.
Reports and identifying details are shared only as needed to investigate and
enforce this policy.
